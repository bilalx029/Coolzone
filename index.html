FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

How does Daikin Inverter save electricity?
Daikin's inverter compressor adjusts speed instead of switching on/off, slashing power bills by up to 65% while maintaining steady comfort.
What is Daikin's Coanda Airflow?
Curved louvers direct cool air along the ceiling so it cascades down gently — no cold drafts, just even room cooling from corner to corner.
What warranty do I get on Daikin ACs?
All units come with 1–5 year comprehensive warranty plus an industry-leading 10-year compressor warranty through Coolzone authorized service.
Can I control my AC via smartphone?
Built-in Wi-Fi connects to the Daikin Home app on iOS & Android. Works with Alexa and Google Assistant voice commands too.
